# Employee Benefits

## Overview
The company provides a comprehensive benefits package to all full-time employees. Contract and part-time employees may be eligible for a subset of these benefits.

## Health Insurance
All employees and their immediate family members (spouse and up to 2 children) are covered under the company's group health insurance plan. The policy covers hospitalisation, day-care procedures, and pre- and post-hospitalisation expenses up to INR 5,00,000 per year.

## Dental Coverage
Basic dental care (cleaning, fillings, extractions) is covered up to INR 10,000 per year. Cosmetic dental procedures are not covered.

## Vision Care
Annual eye examinations and corrective lenses (glasses or contact lenses) are reimbursed up to INR 5,000 per year. Submit receipts to HR within 30 days of purchase.

## Gym and Wellness Allowance
Employees receive a monthly wellness allowance of INR 1,500, which can be used for gym memberships, yoga classes, or other fitness activities. Reimbursement requires a receipt.

## Life Insurance
The company provides a term life insurance cover of 3× the employee's annual CTC, at no cost to the employee.

## Provident Fund (PF)
The company contributes 12% of the employee's basic salary to the Provident Fund, matching the statutory employee contribution.

## Employee Stock Options (ESOPs)
Senior employees (Grade 4 and above) may be eligible for ESOPs based on performance. Details are communicated separately during the annual appraisal.

## Note
The company does not currently offer pet insurance as part of the employee benefits package.
