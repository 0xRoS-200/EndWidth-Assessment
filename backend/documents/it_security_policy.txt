# IT and Security Policy

## Overview
This policy sets out the rules for using company IT systems and handling company data. Compliance is mandatory for all employees, contractors, and interns.

## Acceptable Use
- Company devices (laptops, phones, tablets) are provided for business use. Limited personal use is permitted, provided it does not interfere with work or violate any other policy.
- Accessing, storing, or sharing illegal content on company devices is strictly prohibited.
- Employees must not install unlicensed software on company devices.

## Password Policy
- All account passwords must be at least 12 characters and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Do not reuse any of the last 10 passwords.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts including email, HR portal, and code repositories.

## Data Handling
- Confidential company data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox).
- Customer data is classified as "Restricted" and must only be accessed on a need-to-know basis.
- All data transfers outside the company network must use encrypted channels (HTTPS, SFTP, or VPN).

## Remote Access
- VPN must be used when accessing internal systems from outside the office network.
- Employees must lock their screen when stepping away from their workstation, especially in public spaces.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, data breach, or lost/stolen device must be reported to IT Security (security@company.internal) within 1 hour of discovery.

## Software Updates
- Operating system and security patches must be applied within 48 hours of release.
- The IT team may push updates remotely; employees must not defer these updates.
