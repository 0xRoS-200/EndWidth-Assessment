# Business Travel Policy

## Overview
This policy governs all travel undertaken by employees on behalf of the company. All business travel must be pre-approved before bookings are made.

## Approval Process
1. Submit a travel request in the HR portal at least 5 working days before the intended departure date.
2. Include the purpose of travel, destination, estimated cost, and duration.
3. Approval is required from both the direct manager and the Finance team for trips exceeding INR 50,000.

## Booking
- All flights and hotels must be booked through the company's approved travel vendor (MakeMyTrip Business Account).
- Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be approved for flights over 6 hours or for senior management.
- Hotel bookings should not exceed INR 5,000 per night in Tier-1 cities and INR 3,000 per night in other locations.

## Daily Allowance (Per Diem)
- Domestic travel: INR 1,500 per day for meals and local transport.
- International travel: USD 80 per day.
- Per diem is paid as a flat allowance and does not require receipts.

## Reimbursement
- Expenses beyond the per diem (e.g., client dinner) must be supported by original receipts and submitted within 7 working days of returning.
- Reimbursement requests are processed within 10 working days of submission.

## Personal Travel Extensions
Employees may extend a business trip for personal travel at their own cost, but the return date must be clearly marked as personal and not claimed for reimbursement.
